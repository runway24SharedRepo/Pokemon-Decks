Here is the full key you can use for your reference:

[C] = Colorless (This is a wildcard. Any type of Energy can be used to pay for a Colorless cost).

[R] = Fire Energy

[W] = Water Energy

[G] = Grass Energy

[L] = Lightning Energy

[P] = Psychic Energy

[F] = Fighting Energy

[D] = Darkness Energy

[M] = Metal Energy (Note: Although it's technically "Metal" in the TCG, Energy cards are often printed as 
"Steel" type, but the symbol is M).
