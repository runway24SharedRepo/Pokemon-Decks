Legacy Energy (Special Energy) – x1

Effect: As long as this card is attached to a Pokémon, it provides every type of Energy but provides only 1 
Energy at a time. If the Pokémon this card is attached to is Knocked Out, that player draws a card until they 
have 6 Prize cards remaining. The effect of your Legacy Energy can't be applied more than once at a time.

Bottom Card Details: You may play any number of Energy cards during your turn. You can't attach more than 1 
Energy card to a Pokémon during your turn.
